# Asena Beshiktepeli – portfolio site

Source of my personal portfolio site: https://asenabeshiktepeli.github.io

I'm an e-commerce virtual assistant and customer support specialist: I help e-commerce businesses answer customers faster, track orders clearly and keep their data clean.

## Sample projects

All built with fictional data to show how I work.

- [Customer Request Automation (n8n)](automation/n8n/): a request form with automated follow-up in Asana, Google Calendar and Google Sheets. (Sample project, fictional data)
- [AI shopping assistant readiness check](https://asenabeshiktepeli.github.io/ai-ready/): a 10-question self-check for small online shops, with a score, the top 3 gaps and plain-language policy templates. (Sample project)
- [Order Tracking Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1qmdii_crHkBCz38AoZM4wB0z7KvREubd9FTeGDE5Ieg/preview): a Google Sheets tracker showing each order's status, shipping date and follow-up notes. (Sample project, fictional data)
- [AI-Assisted Reply Drafting](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1uEHgSsQIdq1cHJ-TXXhwg54oFaYkwxCzJ63wrU6IX64/preview): how I use AI for first drafts of customer replies and check each one against store policy before it is sent. (Sample project, fictional data)
- [Customer Support Response Library](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1xYm1fliueMGKPDli2UzKeNNLda1rVmu8jLQrLuVwvgo/preview): a support playbook for a small online shop: 10 reply templates with response time targets and escalation rules. (Sample project, fictional data)
- [Data Cleanup Before/After](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15lbhsX5VS6nrGrRYlrYHuI1Cgy2hru-fDLFgh4He9sY/preview): a messy customer export cleaned with reusable formulas, with every change logged and checked. (Sample project, fictional data)
- [HubSpot Support Workflow](https://asenabeshiktepeli.github.io/#work-hubspot-support): a practice HubSpot help desk for a fictional online shop: 8 tickets moved through a pipeline and tracked on a dashboard. (Sample project, fictional data)
- [Jira Partner Support Desk](https://asenabeshiktepeli.github.io/#work-jira-support): a practice Jira Service Management desk for fictional bank and payment partners, with SLA goals for first response and resolution. (Sample project, fictional data)
- [Black Friday & Christmas support checklist](https://asenabeshiktepeli.github.io/black-friday-support/): a short checklist and three reply templates for small online shops in the busy season. (Sample project)

## Sample: Black Friday & Christmas support checklist

`content.yaml` (all text) + `templates/` (Jinja2) → `build.py` (Python 3) → `docs/`, published with GitHub Pages.

Black Friday & Christmas support checklist for small online shops

The busy weeks bring the same messages again and again: where is my order, why is delivery late, how do I return this. A little preparation now saves hours later.

Checklist

  1. 1Write down your promotions, shipping times and return rules in one place that anyone helping you can read.
  2. 2List the problems from last season (delays, out-of-stock items, payment errors) and draft replies for them now.
  3. 3Make contact easy: show your email and expected reply time in the footer and in order emails.
  4. 4Update your FAQ with current shipping and return details and link to it from order emails.
  5. 5Warn customers early about known delays or low stock.
  6. 6Check that tracking emails really arrive and the links work.
  7. 7Keep returns simple: one clear page, one clear step.
  8. 8Plan extra hands for the peak days and brief them on your brand voice.

Sample reply templates (not from a real customer)

Where is my order:

"Hi [name], thank you for your patience. Your order [order number] was sent on [date] with [carrier]. Here is your tracking link: [link]. During the busy season delivery can take a few extra days. If it has not arrived by [date], reply to this email and we will look into it right away."

Delivery delay:

"Hi [name], we wanted to let you know that your order [order number] is running a little late because of high demand. The new estimate is [date]. We are sorry for the wait and will update you if anything changes."

Return request:

"Hi [name], we are sorry the item was not right. To start a return, please [step]. Once we receive it, we will [refund or exchange] within [time]."

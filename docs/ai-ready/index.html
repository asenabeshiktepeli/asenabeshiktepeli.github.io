    How to fix each point

    One short fix for each question, in the same order.

    1. 1Shipping page

      Write one shipping page with three facts in plain sentences: where you ship, what it costs and how many business days delivery takes.

    2. 2Returns page

      Put three facts near the top of your returns page: the return window in days, who pays for return shipping, and how and when refunds are paid.

    3. 3Late orders

      Add a short section on late orders: when an order counts as late, what you do about it and how customers can reach you.

    4. 4Damaged items

      Explain the steps for a damaged item: how many days customers have to report it, that a photo helps, and whether they get a replacement or a refund.

    5. 5FAQ as text

      Put your FAQ on a normal page as text, with one question per heading. Images and PDFs can stay as extras, but not as the only place the answers live.

    6. 6Same facts everywhere

      Pick one delivery time, one return window and one price for each item, and use them on every page. An assistant may repeat whichever version it finds.

    7. 7Clear product titles

      Start each title with what the item is, then the key details, e.g. “Linen Cushion Cover, 45 × 45 cm, Sage Green” instead of “Nordic Dream”.

    8. 8Complete product basics

      Give every product a price, a stock status, at least one photo and a description of two sentences or more. Start with your best sellers.

    9. 9Material, size and care

      Fill in material, dimensions and care instructions for each product, as separate fields or as clear lines in the description.

    10. 10Sizes and colours

      Set sizes and colours up as options of one product, and use one spelling for each value, e.g. “Sage Green” everywhere.
